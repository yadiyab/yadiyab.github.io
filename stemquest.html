STEMQuest

A gamified mobile app that helps STEMpower’s Fablab reach more young learners and show off its science kits.

ProjectFinal project, LXD Academy
ClientSTEMpower Fablab department
TeamYeabsra Dawit, Nahom Solomon, Mohammed Hussien, Dawit Yared
Facilitators: Zelalem Zewdie, Abenet Markos
My roleUX Designer & Researcher. I took the project from research to the final prototype and pitched the ideas.
Result3rd place, UX Design certificate (Dereja)

The problem

STEMpower is well known in STEM education, but its Fablab department, which makes science kits for children and high school students, was much less visible. Our team was partnered with Fablab to close that gap.

1

Fablab had far less visibility and public awareness than the wider STEMpower organization.

2

There were marketing gaps in how the science kits were promoted and distributed.

3

There was a chance to design a better experience for people visiting STEMpower online.

Who we designed for

LearnersChildren and high school students who want interactive, curiosity-driven STEM experiences.
StakeholdersFablab (mainly Ruhama), parents and educators, who want more engagement, visibility and access for the kits.

How we worked

We used the empathy-driven methods from our LXD training:

  • Initial meetings and interviews, mainly with Ruhama from Fablab
  • A visibility and outreach gap analysis
  • Learner and stakeholder persona development
  • Competitive scans of educational engagement models
  • Ideation and validation of user-centered solutions

The solution

“Every child has a scientist inside them.”

STEMQuest is a mobile app built around that idea. It turns learning into a quest, and it puts Fablab’s kits in front of the learners who would love them.

  • Gamified educational quests to keep learners curious and coming back
  • Project sharing and online events so learners can show their work
  • “What’s New in STEM” section to keep content fresh
  • Science Kit Marketplace to showcase and sell Fablab’s kits
  • Interactive lessons that match the kit content

The impact we aimed for

We believe STEMQuest can improve engagement, visibility and accessibility for Fablab’s offerings: motivating students, reinforcing their curiosity and showcasing the kits more effectively.

What I learned

This was my first UX project, and I loved it. Our biggest lesson as a team was the power of human-centered design and prototyping. Hands-on methods taught us to understand and empathize with the people we design for, and reflecting on our own process helped us grow as learning designers.
