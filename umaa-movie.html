UMAA Movie

A mobile movie app concept I designed in Figma. UMAA stands for Unlimited Movies Anytime Anywhere.

TypePersonal design experiment (concept)
RoleUI/UX Designer, solo
ToolFigma

The idea

This started as an experiment: I wanted to design a full mobile flow from the first screen to the last, using a movie app as the subject. It’s a concept, not a real product, and the posters are there to make the screens feel real.

What I designed

The app takes a viewer from signing up to finding something to watch:

  • Landing page with sign in and sign up, plus a tagline built from the app’s name
  • Sign up, sign in and email verification
  • Category picker (“choose what you love”) with animation, horror, comedy, action, romance and sci-fi
  • Home with highlights, new movies and coming soon
  • Movie details with rating, synopsis, images from the movie and a “Watch now” button
  • Favorites, profile and search
